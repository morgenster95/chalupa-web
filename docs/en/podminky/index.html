Terms of stay

Check-in / Check-out

Check-in: 15:00 · Check-out: 10:00

Payment

For an enquiry you pay by bank transfer (QR payment). A 30% deposit is due within 5 days of confirmation, the balance on arrival at the latest. With instant booking you pay the full amount by card.

Cancellation policy

Cancellation more than 30 days before arrival: full refund of the deposit. Cancellation 30–14 days before arrival: 50% of the deposit refunded. Cancellation less than 14 days before arrival: the deposit is non-refundable. If we re-let the dates, we will refund the deposit.

Pets

Unfortunately, pets are not allowed.

Personal data

We use your data only to handle your booking and communicate with you. Card payments are processed by Stripe – your card details never reach us.
